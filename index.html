How Reselling with Weave 365 Works

From selecting a Varanasi weave to collecting your profit, our platform streamlines every single touchpoint.

STEP 01

Register Free

Create your free reseller account in under 60 seconds with ₹0 upfront fee or subscription.

STEP 02

Choose Products

Browse ready-to-sell Banarasi sarees, suits, lehengas and dupattas.

STEP 03

Add Your Margin

Set your markup on top of the wholesale price to determine your customer price and profit.

STEP 04

Create & Share

Create a white-label catalogue and share it with your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or your own website.

STEP 05

Get Customer Order

Your customer places an order with you and transfers payment directly to your account.

STEP 06

Place with Weave 365

Submit the customer's order and shipping details, then pay the Weave 365 base price.

STEP 07

We Fulfil

We inspect, pack the order in plain, unbranded packaging with your brand name, and ship it directly to your customer.

STEP 08

You Keep Your Profit

Your customer price minus the Weave 365 base price is your immediate gross reseller margin.

Reseller FAQs

Transparent answers to all your questions about registration, margins, orders, unboxing, and fulfilment.

Yes. For reselling and dropshipping, you can place single-piece orders according to your customer's requirement without maintaining inventory. This is distinct from regular wholesale buying, where separate MOQs apply.

Weave 365 charges you the wholesale base price shown on the platform. You decide your customer selling price by adding your desired margin. The difference between your customer price and the Weave 365 price is your gross margin.
